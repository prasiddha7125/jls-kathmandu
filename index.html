About Us

A decade of sending students from Kathmandu to Japan.

JLS Kathmandu started in 2014 with one goal: make the process of studying in Japan clear and honest. No inflated promises. No hidden fees. Just a straight path from your first inquiry to your first day of class in Japan.

Our team includes JLPT-certified instructors, former exchange students, and licensed visa consultants who worked directly with Japanese immigration processes. You get advice from people who know the system, not a call center reading a script.

What We Do

Every service you need, under one roof.

You get one point of contact from your first meeting to your first paycheck in Japan.

01

Language School Placement

We match you with accredited Japanese language schools based on your budget, city preference, and intake date. We handle enrollment paperwork directly with the school.

02

University & Vocational Admission

We prepare your application, statement of purpose, and academic transcripts for universities and senmon gakko programs across Japan.

03

Visa Filing & Documentation

Our licensed consultants file your Certificate of Eligibility and student visa application, and correct errors before immigration sees them.

04

JLPT & Japanese Language Prep

Structured classes from N5 to N2, taught by instructors who studied in Japan. You build the language skills schools and employers ask for.

05

Housing & Arrival Support

We arrange your first accommodation, airport pickup, and city registration so your first week in Japan is not spent guessing.

06

Part-Time Work & Career Guidance

We explain your work-hour limits under a student visa and connect you with employers who regularly hire international students.
